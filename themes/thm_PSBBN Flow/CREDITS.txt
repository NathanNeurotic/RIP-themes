PSBBN Flow, for RIPPS2: RiptOPL's PSBBN Flow view, as a theme folder

Perspective cover fan: darkladark (github.com/dnunezx), from LUNA's Collection display (github.com/dnunezx/LUNA),
contributed to RiptOPL in Open-PS2-Loader PR #733. The fan is drawn by the engine (type=PSBBNFlow), not shipped here.
The look follows the PSBBN Definitive Project's Game Collection (CosmicScale,
github.com/CosmicScale/PSBBN-Definitive-Project).
Layout: the RiptOPL project's built-in theme_psbbn_flow.cfg (github.com/NathanNeurotic/RiptOPL, misc/), kept as
written except its font1=builtin line, which names RiptOPL's built-in font and does nothing in a folder theme.
Port to a theme folder: NathanNeurotic (Ripto), 11/10/2026, from RiptOPL rebuild/main d74f504.
psbbn_bg.png: the background RiptOPL ships for this view. Its maker is not recorded in RiptOPL's history.
The other pictures (cover, app cover, busy spinner, buttons, favourite marks) are RiptOPL's IncebtIon-edition
art by Berion.
