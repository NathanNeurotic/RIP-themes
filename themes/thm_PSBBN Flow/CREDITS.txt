PSBBN Flow: RiptOPL's PSBBN Flow view, as a theme folder

Perspective cover fan: darkladark (github.com/dnunezx), from LUNA's Collection display (github.com/dnunezx/LUNA),
contributed to RiptOPL in Open-PS2-Loader PR #733. The fan is drawn by the engine (type=PSBBNFlow).
The look follows the PSBBN Definitive Project's Game Collection (CosmicScale,
github.com/CosmicScale/PSBBN-Definitive-Project).
Layout: the RiptOPL project's built-in theme_psbbn_flow.cfg (github.com/NathanNeurotic/RiptOPL, misc/), kept as
written except its font1=builtin line, which names RiptOPL's built-in font and does nothing in a folder theme.
Port to a theme folder: NathanNeurotic (Ripto), 11/10/2026, from RiptOPL rebuild/main 9bd25cb.
psbbn_bg.png: the background RiptOPL ships for this view. Its maker is not recorded in RiptOPL's history.
The other pictures are RiptOPL's IncebtIon-edition art by Berion.
Font: PoeVeticaNew.ttf is POE Vetica New by Dan P. Lyons, copyright The Powerhouse of Entertainment, Inc. 2016;
POE Vetica New is a registered trademark of The Powerhouse of Entertainment, Inc. It is the face RiptOPL embeds,
shipped here unchanged so the theme reads as it does in RiptOPL.
Sounds: sound/*.adp are RiptOPL's own eight sounds (audio/ in RiptOPL). Music: sound/bgm.ogg is the track RiptOPL's
release ships beside the ELF (APP_RIPTOPL/bgm.ogg); its maker is not recorded in RiptOPL's history.
