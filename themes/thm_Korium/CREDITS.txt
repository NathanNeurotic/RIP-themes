Korium: RiptOPL's Korium edition look, as a theme folder

Graphics: Berion. Theme script (Korium Komblete): KrahJohlito and Ripto.
Layout: the Korium edition's built-in conf_theme_OPL.cfg (github.com/NathanNeurotic/RiptOPL, third_party/korium/),
kept as written, its own comments included.
Port to a theme folder: NathanNeurotic (Ripto), 11/10/2026, from RiptOPL rebuild/main 9bd25cb.
Ported with Berion's agreement and NathanNeurotic's.
The images were made 8-bit palette PNGs with tools/png8.py, as this kit asks; most convert exactly, the busy
spinner, disc and button glyphs lose a little (worst channel error 16 of 255, mean under 1).
Font: PoeVeticaNew.ttf is POE Vetica New by Dan P. Lyons, copyright The Powerhouse of Entertainment, Inc. 2016;
POE Vetica New is a registered trademark of The Powerhouse of Entertainment, Inc. It is the face RiptOPL embeds,
shipped here unchanged so the theme reads as it does in RiptOPL.
Sounds: sound/*.adp are RiptOPL's own eight sounds (audio/ in RiptOPL). Music: sound/bgm.ogg is the track RiptOPL's
release ships beside the ELF (APP_RIPTOPL/bgm.ogg); its maker is not recorded in RiptOPL's history.
